# About

This website is for the company Design Quest Solutions (DQS for short). It's a woman owned business that is a consulting company that specializes in building BIM (building information models) models in Revit for HVAC and Plumbing engineering for commercial buildings.

There are 2 owners that have 25+ years of industry experience and were early adopters of Revit and are now experts.
Their main differentiator is that they produce extremely high quality work and focus on meticulously crafting models that are easy to use and understand and follow national CAD standards.

DQS focuses on the Los Angeles county area, and is familiar with state and local code and can help catch errors while building models.
Their main clients are architects and general contractors. Specifically, DQS, gets markup drawings as PDFs from engineers that work with the clients, and converts them into live 3D Revit models that help identify clash detection or any other building issues before actual construction begins and before plans are submitted to the city.
