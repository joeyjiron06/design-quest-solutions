# The Drafting Company

- **Website:** https://thedraftingcompany.com
- **LA page:** https://thedraftingcompany.com/los-angeles-office
- **Listed LA address:** 11601 Wilshire Blvd, Los Angeles, CA 90025
- **Contact:** (800) 504-2658, sales@thedraftingcompany.com
- **Reviewed:** 2026-09-23

## What they sell

A full reality-capture-to-deliverable production pipeline.

- **Reality capture and scanning:** site laser scanning, scan-to-BIM, laser scanning to 3D,
  scan-to-plan
- **MEP BIM modelling and shop drawings** — the one line closest to DQS's work
- **BIM and coordination:** Revit as-built, BIM Revit modelling, BIM modelling, BIM as-built,
  laser scanning to BIM
- **Commercial scan-to-BIM and existing facility documentation**
- **CAD drafting and conversion:** scan-to-CAD, CAD remastering, paper and PDF to CAD
  conversion, CAD conversion, laser scanning to CAD

## How they position themselves

- A national footprint marketed as local. The site lists **26 city "locations"**: Atlanta,
  Boston, Chicago, Dallas, Denver, Fargo, Honolulu, Houston, Jersey City, Las Vegas, Los
  Angeles, Miami, Milwaukee, Minneapolis, Nashville, New York, Omaha, Orlando, Philadelphia,
  Phoenix, San Diego, San Francisco, Seattle, St. Louis, Tampa Bay and Washington DC.
- A single toll-free 800 number serves every "location".
- The primary calls to action are "Contact" and "Request a Quote".

## ⚠️ Read the local claim carefully

Twenty-six offices, one 800 number, and no named local staff is the signature of a **SEO
location-page strategy**, not 26 real offices. Treat "Los Angeles office" as a marketing page
until proven otherwise. The practical implication: a client who thinks they are hiring a
neighbour is likely being routed to a central or offshore production team.

## Strengths

- Wide service menu, so they can answer almost any drafting or scanning request.
- Strong search presence across many cities, which means they show up whenever an LA buyer
  searches.
- **They do list MEP BIM modelling and shop drawings**, which makes them one of the few
  search-visible firms overlapping DQS's actual service.

## Weaknesses and gaps

- **No people, no proof.** No named team, no leadership, no project case studies, no
  testimonials surfaced on the main pages.
- **No local substance.** No LA project list, no California code knowledge, no plan-check
  experience.
- **MEP is one line in a long menu.** No HVAC or plumbing depth, no evidence of engineering
  judgement.
- **Scan-led, not design-led.** The business is organised around capturing existing conditions,
  not around turning engineer markups into a new design model.
- **No stated quality standard.** Nothing about CAD standards, model organisation or usability.

## What DQS can take from this

This is the clearest example of a competitor DQS can beat on authenticity. The Drafting Company
claims to be everywhere; DQS actually is somewhere. Lean into it:

- Name the owners and their 25+ years of experience. This competitor has no faces at all.
- Show LA projects and LA code knowledge.
- Point out, politely, what a real local partner means: someone who can sit in a coordination
  meeting, knows the plan checker's expectations, and answers the phone in Pacific time.
