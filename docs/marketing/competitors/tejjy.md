# Tejjy Inc.

> **Most important competitor in this file set.** Tejjy is a certified woman-owned BIM firm.
> They already occupy the positioning DQS is considering, at national scale.

- **Website:** https://www.tejjy.com
- **California page:** https://www.tejjy.com/bim-services-california/
- **Location:** Headquartered in Maryland. Serves the US nationally with offshore production
  capacity.
- **Founded:** 2006. Claims 18+ years of experience.
- **Reviewed:** 2026-09-23

## Certifications — the headline

Tejjy markets itself as an **8(a) Certified Women-Owned Small Business**, carrying:

- WOSB (Women-Owned Small Business)
- MBE (Minority Business Enterprise)
- DBE (Disadvantaged Business Enterprise)
- WBE (Women's Business Enterprise)
- SDVOSB affiliation is also referenced
- Listed on the **GSA schedule**

They run a dedicated "WOSB MBE SDVOSB Certified BIM Firm" white paper and a "Federal BIM
Services For GSA, USACE & VA" campaign. The certification is not a footnote for them — it is a
lead-generation engine aimed at federal and public set-aside work.

## What they sell

- **BIM:** coordination, scan-to-BIM, Revit family creation, shop drawings, CAD-to-BIM, 4D
  simulation, 5D cost estimation, preconstruction
- **Discipline BIM:** MEP BIM services, MEP coordination, MEP drawings, structural BIM, steel
  shop drawings, architectural BIM, facade shop drawings
- **3D laser scanning:** interior and exterior, historical laser scanning
- **Geospatial and GIS:** utility and infrastructure mapping, LiDAR/point cloud to GIS,
  geospatial digital twins
- **Engineering and design:** 2D/3D floor plans, as-builts, commercial and residential design,
  CAD drafting, VDC, government and federal facilities
- **Consulting and management:** BIM consulting and implementation, constructability review,
  construction management, construction documentation, facilities operations, asset management,
  **permit expediting**

## Proof points they publish

- 2,500+ completed projects
- 150 million square feet delivered
- Multidisciplinary team of architects, engineers, MEP consultants and BIM modellers
- Founded as a concrete cutting company, transitioned into CAD, BIM and digital twins

## Industries

Mission critical, healthcare, civic infrastructure, education and institutional, commercial,
semiconductor, energy and infrastructure, government and federal.

## Strengths

- **The certification stack is a moat for public work.** Federal, state and municipal buyers
  with small-business or WBE participation goals can route work to them with minimal
  justification.
- Enormous service breadth, including permit expediting and constructability review.
- Long operating history and large claimed volume.

## Weaknesses and gaps

- **Not local to Los Angeles.** Maryland-based with an SEO-driven California landing page. No LA
  office, no LA project list, no California code specificity beyond generic copy.
- **Federal and East Coast centre of gravity.** GSA, USACE and VA are their named targets.
- **Offshore production.** Cost-efficient, but it is the model TITAN AEC and other LA firms
  attack directly.
- **Breadth dilutes the craft story.** Nothing communicates that an individual model is
  meticulously built. The proof points are volume metrics, not quality metrics.
- **Site copy quality is rough.** Visible typos ("18years", "transformaion", "gands concrete
  cutting"). Small, but it undercuts a precision claim.

## ⚠️ What this means for DQS

**"Woman-owned" alone is not a differentiator.** Tejjy has been running that play nationally for
years, with stronger certifications and a federal channel. If DQS leads with woman-owned, it
competes on Tejjy's terms and loses on credentials.

The better strategy is to combine attributes Tejjy cannot match:

| Attribute | Tejjy | DQS opportunity |
|---|---|---|
| Woman-owned | Yes, heavily certified | Yes — but get certified, do not just claim it |
| Los Angeles local | No | Yes |
| California / LA code knowledge | Generic | Deep and specific |
| HVAC + plumbing specialists | One line in a large menu | The entire business |
| Work performed onshore | Offshore production | US-based, named experts |
| Named senior experts | Not surfaced | Two owners, 25+ years each |

**Recommended actions:**

1. **Pursue formal certification** (WBENC WBE, California SB/DVBE portal, LA Metro/LAUSD/LA City
   SBE programmes). An unverified "woman-owned" claim is worth far less than a certificate
   number that a public agency can look up.
2. **Never lead with woman-owned alone.** Lead with HVAC and plumbing expertise and LA code
   knowledge. Let the certification be the tiebreaker that unlocks public projects.
