# MAX 360

- **Website:** https://www.max360.co
- **BIM services page:** https://www.max360.co/bim-services/
- **Location:** Not disclosed on the pages reviewed. The site is structured around outsourcing,
  which usually indicates offshore or nearshore production.
- **Reviewed:** 2026-09-23

## What they sell

- Virtual Design & Construction (VDC)
- BIM services, BIM coordination, BIM production, BIM content creation
- Detailing and shop drawings
- Scan-to-BIM
- BIM for prefabrication
- CAD drafting
- Quantity takeoffs
- Renderings and visualisations

## How they position themselves

The site is organised by **client type**, and every one of those pages is literally named
"outsourcing":

- Outsourcing Solutions for Architects
- Outsourcing Solutions for General Contractors
- Outsourcing Services for Trade Contractors
- Outsourcing Services for Owners/Developers
- Outsourcing Services for Building Product Manufacturers and Suppliers

There is also a "How We Work" section and a "Why MAX 360" page, with "Schedule A Call" and "Get
Started" as the calls to action.

## Who they target

General contractors, trade contractors, architects, engineers, owners and developers, and
manufacturers and suppliers.

## Strengths

- **Audience-segmented navigation.** Letting a visitor self-select ("I'm an architect") is
  effective and worth imitating.
- **BIM for prefabrication** is a differentiated offering. It matters to mechanical and plumbing
  trade contractors who spool and prefab off the model.
- Clean, modern site presentation.

## Weaknesses and gaps

- **"Outsourcing" is the whole brand.** That frames the relationship as cost arbitrage, not
  partnership. It also concedes the local and onshore ground entirely.
- **No location, no named people, no local credibility.** Nothing anchors them to a place.
- **No MEP discipline depth.** Mechanical and plumbing are not called out as specialities.
- **No code, plan-check or quality-standard claims.**
- Generic capability list with no proof of judgement or expertise.

## What DQS can take from this

Two ideas are worth borrowing, one is worth rejecting:

**Borrow — audience-segmented pages.** DQS's two main buyers are architects and general
contractors, and they have different problems. Give each a page:

- *For Architects* — turn your engineer's PDF markups into a coordinated model, catch code and
  clash issues before you submit.
- *For General Contractors* — get a mechanical and plumbing model your trades can actually build
  from.

**Borrow — prefabrication.** If DQS's HVAC and plumbing models are clean enough to support
spooling and prefab, that is a concrete, high-value claim few local firms make.

**Reject — the word "outsourcing."** DQS is not a cheaper pair of hands. It is a specialist
partner. The language should reflect that.
