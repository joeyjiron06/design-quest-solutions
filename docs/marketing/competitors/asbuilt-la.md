# As-Built LA

- **Website:** https://asbuiltla.com
- **Location:** California-based, serving nationally. Phone (818) 208-3616 — a San Fernando
  Valley number.
- **Reviewed:** 2026-09-23

## What they sell

A narrow, high-volume as-built service.

- As-builts for architecture
- As-builts for interior design
- Deliverables in AutoCAD, Revit and SketchUp
- On-site measurement and floor plan capture

## How they position themselves

- Headline: **"The Fastest As Built Service in Los Angeles."** Speed is the entire proposition.
- Secondary claims: *"highest ratings"*, *"clean and accurate"* files.
- **Same-day quote** is the primary call to action, repeated throughout the page.
- **Published pricing guide.** They are one of the very few firms in this market willing to talk
  about price openly.
- Heavy use of Google-style customer reviews, including reviews from non-expert clients going
  through remodels.
- Self-serve sample files: downloadable Revit, SketchUp and AutoCAD samples so buyers can judge
  quality before committing.

## Who they target

Architects and interior designers first. The review content also shows office managers,
homeowners and remodel clients, so the base skews small and fast-moving.

## Strengths

- **Removes friction.** Same-day quotes, published pricing, downloadable samples. A buyer can
  self-qualify in ten minutes.
- **Owns one word: fast.** Easy to remember and easy to refer.
- Strong, recent, specific review volume.
- The samples page is a genuinely smart trust device.

## Weaknesses and gaps

- **No MEP work whatsoever.** No HVAC, no plumbing, no coordination, no clash detection.
- **Commodity positioning.** Competing on speed and price invites a race to the bottom and
  attracts clients who will leave for the next cheapest option.
- **Small and simple projects.** Nothing suggests they can handle a commercial building's
  mechanical systems.
- **No engineering credibility.** No stated code knowledge, no licensed or long-tenured
  discipline experts.

## What DQS can take from this

Two tactics are worth stealing, even though As-Built LA is not a direct competitor:

1. **Publish sample deliverables.** Let an architect open a real DQS Revit model and see the
   craftsmanship. For a firm whose differentiator is quality, showing the work beats describing
   it.
2. **Make quoting easy.** A clear intake path — send your PDF markups, get a scoped quote —
   removes the biggest hesitation for a first-time client.

DQS should *not* copy the speed-and-price framing. That is the opposite of a meticulous-quality
brand.
