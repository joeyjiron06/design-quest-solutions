# TITAN AEC (Nexus AEC LLC)

- **Website:** https://www.titanaec.com
- **LA page:** https://www.titanaec.com/los-angeles/
- **Location:** Downtown Los Angeles (725 S Figueroa St). Covers LA, Orange, Riverside and San Diego counties.
- **Reviewed:** 2026-09-23

## What they sell

TITAN AEC positions itself as a BIM and VDC consultancy for the whole AECO industry, not a
drafting shop. Their service list is broad:

- BIM services for design teams; VDC services for general contractors and trade contractors
- Clash detection and BIM coordination
- Discipline modelling: MEP, fire protection, structural steel, concrete and rebar, framing and
  drywall, site utilities, signage
- Laser scanning, reality capture and scan-to-BIM
- BIM project management, standards development, BIM content development
- Technology implementation, IT advisory, API development, Dynamo and Grasshopper scripting
- Staff augmentation and project shadowing or mentoring
- Autodesk, Trimble, Bluebeam, Rhino, Synchro 4D, Unity and Unreal training
- Data analytics, 4D planning, VR and MR, floor flatness verification

## How they position themselves

- **"100% based in the United States of America."** They repeat this on every page and state
  plainly: *"TITAN AEC does NOT off-shore any work."* This is their headline differentiator.
- **"TITAN AEC is NOT a software reseller."** They separate themselves from Autodesk resellers
  while still naming partnerships with Autodesk, ESRI, Microsoft, Faro, Unity and Unreal.
- **Marquee LA projects.** Wilshire Grand Redevelopment, Metropolis, SoFi / LA Rams Stadium,
  Port of Los Angeles, LAX.
- **Process and technology depth** rather than production output. The pitch is "we make your
  workflow better", not "we draw your sheets".
- Claims "over 30 years of cumulative industry experience" across the team.

## Who they target

General contractors, trade contractors, design teams and owner/operators on large commercial,
civic and infrastructure projects.

## Strengths

- Genuine DTLA headquarters and a credible LA project list.
- The strongest "no offshoring" message of any firm reviewed. That message is available to DQS
  too, and it clearly resonates in this market.
- Training and technology advisory give them recurring revenue and a reason to stay in front of
  clients between projects.

## Weaknesses and gaps

- **Very wide, not deep.** Seventeen service lines. Nothing signals that HVAC and plumbing are a
  specialty rather than one item on a menu.
- **Enterprise and mega-project bias.** A mid-size commercial building or a tenant improvement
  is unlikely to feel like their sweet spot.
- **Site looks stale.** The footer reads "© 2022 Nexus AEC LLC" and the events list stops at
  CACX 2022. Either the firm has slowed down or its marketing has. Worth verifying before
  treating them as an active threat.
- **No code or plan-check story.** Nothing about California Title 24, LA City or LA County plan
  check, or catching errors before submittal.
- **No quality standard named.** They never mention national CAD standards, model usability, or
  what "good" looks like in a deliverable.

## What DQS can take from this

They have already proven the "US-based, local, not offshore" message works in LA. DQS can match
it and go further: add 25+ years of *hands-on HVAC and plumbing* experience, LA-specific code
knowledge, and a named quality standard. Where TITAN is a mile wide, DQS can be the specialist
that architects and GCs call when the mechanical and plumbing model has to be right.
