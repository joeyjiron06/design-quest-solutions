# Outside the Box (Design & Drafting Services, LLC)

- **Website:** https://www.design-otb.com
- **BIM page:** https://www.design-otb.com/services/bim-services
- **Location:** Headquartered in Santa Rosa, California (1275 4th St. #3405). Markets Los
  Angeles and San Diego as service locations. Phone 707-495-8950 — a Sonoma County number.
- **Reviewed:** 2026-09-23

## What they sell

A small-project design and drafting shop that has added BIM to the menu.

- **BIM:** BIM consulting, 3D BIM modelling, structural BIM, MEP BIM, scan-to-BIM, CAD-to-BIM,
  Revit BIM modelling, 5D BIM
- **Drafting:** CAD drafting, MEP drafting, civil drafting, electrical drafting, CAD conversion
  and digitisation
- **Small-project work:** custom cabinetry shop drawings, custom kitchen cabinetry design, ADU
  design and drafting, residential design and construction drawings, permit drawings, as-built
  drawings, 3D modelling

## How they position themselves

- Four reasons to choose them: expert BIM professionals, high-quality BIM models,
  multi-disciplinary expertise, and serving Santa Rosa / Los Angeles / San Diego.
- Calls to action: "Get a FREE Quote", "Book Your FREE Consultation", "Book Appointment".
- FAQ section aimed at buyers new to BIM, including *"Do you provide BIM services for small
  residential projects?"*

## Who they target

**Homeowners first.** Their audience list reads: *"homeowners, architects, construction
companies, home builders, interior designers, and survey companies."* Listing homeowners before
architects tells you where the revenue comes from.

## Strengths

- Approachable and low-friction. Free quotes, free consultations, plain-language FAQ.
- **Permit drawings** is a real offering and shows some familiarity with approval processes.
- The FAQ format is good for buyers who do not yet know what BIM is.

## Weaknesses and gaps

- **Not a Los Angeles firm.** Santa Rosa is roughly 450 miles north. LA is a landing-page
  location.
- **Residential and small commercial.** ADUs and kitchen cabinetry are not commercial building
  systems. They are unlikely to be bidding against DQS.
- **No commercial MEP depth.** MEP BIM is one bullet among twenty.
- **Free consultations signal a low-value engagement.** That framing attracts price shoppers.

## What DQS can take from this

Outside the Box is a **low-threat competitor** but a useful contrast. They occupy the
residential and homeowner end of the market. DQS occupies the commercial end.

Worth borrowing: **the plain-language FAQ**. Not every architect or GC project manager fully
understands LOD levels, clash detection workflows, or what they actually receive at handover. A
clear, honest FAQ that explains deliverables and process builds trust and supports search
rankings.

Worth avoiding: **free consultations as the main call to action**. For a premium,
expertise-driven brand, "send us your PDF markups and we'll scope it" is a stronger and more
confident invitation.
