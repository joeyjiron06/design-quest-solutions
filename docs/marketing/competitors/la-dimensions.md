# Dimensions (LA Dimensions)

- **Website:** https://www.ladimensions.com
- **Location:** 1835 S La Cienega Blvd, Los Angeles, CA 90035. Phone (323) 487-0140.
- **Reviewed:** 2026-09-23

## What they sell

Dimensions is an existing-conditions and as-built documentation specialist. Everything flows
from capturing a building that is already standing.

- As-built documentation: residential, commercial, industrial
- Landscape site plans
- Reality capture using LiDAR and photogrammetry
- Scan-to-BIM
- BOMA analysis (rentable area calculations)

## How they position themselves

- Headline: **"Nationwide As-Built Drawing Professionals."** Local address, national ambition.
- Technology-forward: *"Harness the power of LiDAR & Photogrammetry to capture Existing
  Conditions during complex Renovations & Compliance Mandates."*
- Named, visible CEO (Adam Kempf) with a quote on the homepage: *"Our clients hire us for our
  energy, customer service and reliability."*
- Proof by numbers: 11 million sq ft captured and modelled, 850 projects completed, 35 ongoing
  projects, 41 team members.
- Social proof: a testimonial from an architect reading *"In my 50+ years in this business, the
  best and most complete documentation I've ever seen."*
- Content marketing: a blog, videos and articles, including commercial real estate market
  commentary.

## Who they target

Architects, commercial real estate owners and managers, and operators across AEC, energy and
utilities, oil and gas, industrial and manufacturing, infrastructure and transport, mining and
surveying, cultural heritage, and emergency response.

## Strengths

- **Clear, single focus.** They do as-builts. The whole site supports one promise.
- **Excellent trust signals.** Named leader, hard numbers, industry testimonials, an
  eight-industry vertical story.
- **Genuinely LA-based** with a real street address and a local phone number.
- Marketing quality is well above the average for this sector.

## Weaknesses and gaps

- **They document what exists. They do not build design-intent models.** There is no service for
  converting engineer markups into a coordinated Revit model.
- **No MEP discipline.** Mechanical, HVAC and plumbing do not appear in the service list at all.
- **No clash detection or coordination offering.** They hand over documentation, not a
  constructability review.
- **No code or plan-check angle.**

## What DQS can take from this

Dimensions is the best marketing example in this set and worth copying structurally: one clear
promise, a named human being, hard numbers, and testimonials from people with credibility.

They are also a **partner, not a rival**. Their work stops where DQS's begins. An architect who
buys a scan-to-BIM as-built from Dimensions still needs someone to model the new HVAC and
plumbing design into it. That is a referral relationship worth pursuing rather than a fight.
