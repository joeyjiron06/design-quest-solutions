# BIM Pro US

- **Website:** https://www.bimprous.com
- **LA page:** https://www.bimprous.com/los-angeles-bim-services/
- **Location:** Not disclosed. No street address or named team found on the pages reviewed.
- **Reviewed:** 2026-09-23

## What they sell

A long, undifferentiated service list:

Revit drafting, architectural drafting, mechanical drafting, electrical drafting, plumbing
drafting, BIM consulting, 3D rendering, architectural BIM, architectural 3D modelling,
structural BIM, Tekla BIM, MEP BIM, mechanical BIM, electrical BIM, plumbing BIM, CAD-to-BIM,
clash detection, BIM coordination, Revit family creation, scan-to-BIM, 4D construction
simulation, shop drawings, PDF-to-BIM conversion.

## How they position themselves

Five generic reasons to choose them, quoted from the LA page:

1. **Expertise in Local Standards** — *"our team is well-versed in the specific BIM requirements
   of Los Angeles region"*
2. **Professional Team**
3. **Results-Driven**
4. **Holistic Approach**
5. **Timely Deliverables**

They claim to serve every phase from schematic design through facility management, and they name
their tools: Revit, Navisworks, AutoCAD, Revizto, Bluebeam.

## ⚠️ The local claim does not hold up

The footer lists state and city landing pages for Arizona, California, Chicago, Colorado,
Connecticut, Dallas, Florida, Georgia, Houston, Kansas, Massachusetts, Nevada, New Hampshire,
New Jersey, New York, North Carolina, Ohio, San Antonio, South Carolina and more.

This is a templated geo-page network. The "Expertise in Local Standards" claim is almost
certainly the same sentence with a different city name swapped in. No LA address, no LA team, no
LA projects, no reference to any actual California code.

## Strengths

- Broad search visibility. They appear near the top for many LA BIM queries.
- They do list mechanical, electrical and plumbing as **separate** services, which shows there
  is search demand for discipline-specific terms.

## Weaknesses and gaps

- **No verifiable identity.** No address, no leadership, no faces.
- **Empty local claim.** Claiming local standards expertise without naming a single code or
  jurisdiction is a weakness DQS can expose simply by being specific.
- **Generic differentiators.** "Results-Driven" and "Holistic Approach" say nothing.
- **No quality standard, no code detail, no plan-check story.**

## What DQS can take from this

This firm ranks well on search and says nothing. That is a large, cheap opportunity.

- **Out-specify them.** Where they write "local standards", DQS can write about Title 24, the
  California Mechanical Code, the California Plumbing Code, LADBS plan check, and the specific
  errors that get plans rejected. Specificity wins both readers and search rankings.
- **Out-prove them.** Named owners, 25+ years of experience, real LA projects, sample models.
- **Copy the page structure, not the content.** Separate pages for mechanical/HVAC BIM and
  plumbing BIM capture the same searches — but with real substance behind them.
