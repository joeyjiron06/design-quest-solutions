# United-BIM Inc.

- **Website:** https://www.united-bim.com
- **LA page:** https://www.united-bim.com/bim-services-in-los-angeles
- **Location:** 60 Darlin Street, Suite 202, East Hartford, CT 06108. Phone (860) 317-7105.
  Markets a "Global Presence" with offshore delivery.
- **Reviewed:** 2026-09-23

## What they sell

The most complete MEP menu in this competitor set, broken down by discipline:

- **MEP outsourcing and modelling:** Mechanical/HVAC modelling, Electrical modelling, **Plumbing
  modelling**, Fire Protection modelling
- **BIM coordination:** Mechanical/HVAC coordination, Electrical coordination, Plumbing
  coordination, clash detection
- **Construction outsourcing:** construction gatekeeping, 4D simulation, 5D cost estimation, LOD
  400 modelling, shop drawings, quantity takeoff
- **Revit family creation:** BIM objects for building product, equipment and furniture
  manufacturers
- Architectural drafting, 3D rendering, electrical design services

**Sectors:** data centre, federal/government, commercial, hospitality, healthcare, residential,
educational, infrastructure.

## How they position themselves

- Brand promise: **"Peace of Mind"** through *Quality, Speed, and a Consultative Approach.*
- Tagline: "For AEC, Through BIM, UNITED-ly".
- **Openly an outsourcing firm.** They publish a page titled "Why Outsource BIM Modeling".
- Three published engagement models:
  - **Fixed price** — for fully defined scope
  - **Time & Material** — hourly rates
  - **Dedicated Team** — retainer, working as the client's extended team
- Pricing page explains the *drivers* (LOD, discipline, building type, square footage, floor
  count, turnaround, deliverables) but publishes **no numbers**. Custom proposal only.
- Trust signals: D-U-N-S number, trade show presence (Autodesk University, New York Build,
  AIA25), downloadable brochure, case studies, checklists and infographics.
- Structured intake form: discipline needed, project type, LOD 100–500, country.

## The LA landing page

Notably better written than most geo-pages. It names real local market drivers: *"airport
modernization, transit expansion, adaptive reuse, high-rise residential, entertainment
facilities, wildfire rebuilding, healthcare campuses, port infrastructure, commercial interiors,
and public-sector upgrades."*

They also publish California case studies — MEP coordination for a 45,000 sq ft school, BIM and
clash coordination for a transit facility.

Even so, there is **no LA office, no LA staff and no California licence or code claim.**

## Strengths

- **Discipline-level menu.** They sell "Plumbing Modeling" as its own product. Very few
  competitors are that specific, and it is exactly DQS's territory.
- Excellent content marketing operation and a credible intake funnel.
- Transparent engagement models reduce buyer risk.
- Real California project case studies.

## Weaknesses and gaps

- **Offshore delivery.** This is the vulnerability the whole local market attacks: time zones,
  communication, rework, and models that technically comply but are painful to work in.
- **No local presence.** A Connecticut address and a Connecticut phone number.
- **Volume over craft.** "Quality" is asserted as a brand word but never defined. No mention of
  national CAD standards, model organisation, or what a well-built model looks like.
- **No code or plan-check capability.** They cannot catch a Title 24 or LA plan-check issue for
  a client.
- **Commodity price pressure.** Their entire premise is that outsourcing is cheaper.

## What DQS can take from this

United-BIM is the **price-anchor competitor**. An architect comparing quotes will likely have
one of these firms in the mix, and DQS will be more expensive.

Prepare a direct answer to "why are you more expensive?":

1. **Total cost, not line-item cost.** Rework, RFIs, failed plan checks and coordination
   meetings cost more than the modelling fee. Quantify it if possible.
2. **Model usability.** A model that follows national CAD standards and is organised logically
   saves the client's own staff time for the life of the project. Offshore volume shops rarely
   deliver this.
3. **Code and constructability review included.** DQS catches errors before submittal. An
   offshore vendor models exactly what the PDF says, mistakes and all.
4. **Same time zone, named people, one phone call.** No overnight round trips on a simple
   question.

Also worth copying: their **discipline-specific page structure**. Separate pages for HVAC
modelling and plumbing modelling will outrank generic "BIM services" copy for the exact searches
DQS's buyers run.
